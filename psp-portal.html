Patient Support Program Discovery Platform

Designed an interactive web-based healthcare platform that helps patients and caregivers discover relevant patient support programs through intelligent metadata filtering. The platform transforms complex healthcare information into an accessible, user-centred discovery experience through intuitive navigation, guided workflows, and personalised recommendations.

OVERVIEW

During this industry project, I designed an end-to-end web-based healthcare platform that enables patients and caregivers to efficiently discover relevant patient support programs. As the sole UX/UI designer and researcher, I led the complete product design process, from literature review and information architecture through wireframing, interaction design, high-fidelity prototyping, and design validation.

The platform balanced user-centred design with complex healthcare requirements by combining guided navigation, metadata-driven filtering, and clear visual communication. Careful attention was given to information hierarchy, accessibility, interaction design, and reducing cognitive load, enabling users to confidently identify programs aligned with their medical needs.

Tools & Methods: Figma, UX Research, Literature Review, Information Architecture, User Flows, Wireframing, High-Fidelity Prototyping, Interaction Design

THE ASK

Patients and caregivers often struggle to find relevant support programs that match their unique needs. Existing resources are scattered and generic, making it difficult to identify programs that truly fit their situation.

THE DESIGN

Designed a responsive web platform that uses metadata-driven filtering and guided questionnaires to transform complex healthcare datasets into personalised recommendations. Clear information architecture, progressive disclosure, and intuitive interaction patterns helped users navigate a large catalogue of patient support programs with minimal cognitive effort.

GOALS & KEY FEATURES

Goals

  • Enable fast, confident discovery of relevant programs
  • Support diverse user groups and abilities

Key Features

RESEARCH

The research phase began with an extensive literature review to understand existing patient support ecosystems, usability challenges, and healthcare information gaps. These findings informed the platform's information architecture, navigation structure, and end-to-end user journey.

Each stage documented the information presented, user goals, interaction requirements, and decision points. This structured approach supported progressive disclosure, improved usability, strengthened visual hierarchy, and reduced cognitive load throughout the discovery experience.

IDEATION & DEVELOPMENT

Low-fidelity wireframes were iteratively developed into interactive prototypes through regular collaboration with Elemetric Health's founder and software engineer. Continuous feedback ensured the interface remained technically feasible, clinically appropriate, and aligned with both user needs and business objectives.

THE DEVELOPMENT

The platform included personalised onboarding flows that identified whether users were patients or caregivers before dynamically tailoring subsequent interactions. Program overview pages presented complex healthcare information using clear visual hierarchy, concise summaries, and guided navigation to support confident decision-making.

Low-fidelity wireframes evolved into high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma, allowing rapid iteration and collaborative design reviews with the engineering team. Design specifications, reusable interface patterns, and consistent interaction behaviours supported efficient implementation and future scalability.

Because the platform operated within a healthcare environment, usability, accessibility, and clarity became primary design objectives. Nielsen's usability heuristics, including visibility of system status, user control and freedom, consistency and standards, and recognition rather than recall, informed interaction design decisions that reduced cognitive load while improving navigation, information discoverability, and overall user confidence.

IMPACT

Patients and caregivers previously faced fragmented information, inconsistent navigation, and significant effort when searching for suitable support programs. I transformed a complex healthcare dataset into a structured, personalised discovery experience that simplified comparison, reduced decision fatigue, and improved access to relevant services.

Through clear information architecture, metadata-driven filtering, and guided user journeys, the platform improved discoverability, reduced cognitive load, and created a scalable foundation for future patient support offerings. The resulting experience demonstrated how thoughtful UX design can translate complex healthcare information into intuitive, accessible digital products.

THE TAKEAWAY

This project strengthened my ability to design user-centred healthcare products that balance complex information, accessibility requirements, and business goals. I developed a deeper appreciation for translating large information ecosystems into intuitive digital experiences while collaborating closely with stakeholders to produce scalable, implementation-ready design solutions.
