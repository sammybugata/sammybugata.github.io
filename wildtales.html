WILDTALES

An educational adventure game that encourages children to explore outdoor environments through playful quests, storytelling, and interactive discovery.

ROLE

Research & UI/UX Design

INDUSTRY

GAME DESIGN

OVERVIEW

Wild Tales is an educational adventure game designed to encourage children to explore outdoor environments through storytelling, play, and discovery. The concept combines mobile interaction with real-world exploration, using narrative quests and collectible experiences to transform outdoor spaces into interactive learning environments.

As the UX/UI designer and researcher on the project, I led the design of the player experience from early research through to high-fidelity prototypes. My role focused on defining the user journey, designing interaction patterns and interface screens, and creating interactive prototypes that communicated the gameplay experience to the development team. The designs were then handed over to front-end and back-end developers for implementation.

The project explored how digital interfaces can motivate real-world exploration while maintaining a sense of wonder, curiosity, and play.

Tools & Methods: Figma, UX Research, User Journeys, Interaction Design, Wireframing, Prototyping, Game UX

THE ASK

Children are spending increasing amounts of time indoors and interacting with screens in passive ways. At the same time, outdoor environments offer powerful opportunities for curiosity, learning, and exploration.

The challenge was to design a digital experience that encourages children to engage with the real world rather than replacing it. The goal was to create a playful system that motivates exploration, rewards curiosity, and turns everyday environments into interactive adventures.

RESEARCH & PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Early research focused on how children interact with digital games and what motivates exploration and discovery. Patterns emerged around the importance of narrative guidance, clear rewards, and playful feedback.

From these insights, I developed a player journey that guides children through exploration-driven gameplay loops. The experience centres around short quests, discoveries, and collectible memories that reinforce a sense of adventure and progress.

The design emphasised simplicity, clarity, and positive reinforcement to ensure the interface remained intuitive and engaging for younger players.

THE DESIGN

The core experience revolves around a narrative companion, Quinn the Quokka, who guides players through quests and discoveries in the environment.

Players complete exploration challenges, uncover hidden items, and collect memories of their adventures in a digital scrapbook. This system transforms everyday locations into playful discovery spaces while reinforcing a sense of achievement and storytelling.

The interface was designed to feel friendly, colourful, and inviting while maintaining clear interaction patterns so children could easily navigate the experience.

Key Design Focus Areas:

  • Narrative-guided exploration
  • Gamified discovery and rewards
  • Clear, playful interface feedback
  • Simple navigation designed for younger users

USER JOURNEY & INTERACTION DESIGN

To support the gameplay loop, I mapped the full player journey from first launch through to ongoing exploration.

This included defining how players:

  • Discover new quests
  • Receive guidance from the companion character
  • Capture discoveries in their personal scrapbook

PROTOTYPING & DEVELOPMENT HANDOFF

Once the core gameplay flow was defined, I translated the experience into interactive Figma prototypes that demonstrated key screens, navigation patterns, and interaction states.

These prototypes allowed the team to test the structure of the game experience and provided a clear blueprint for developers. The final design assets and interaction documentation were then handed over to front-end and back-end developers for implementation.

THE TAKEAWAY

Wild Tales demonstrated how digital design can encourage real-world exploration rather than replacing it. By combining narrative, gamification, and simple interaction design, the project explored how technology can support curiosity, creativity, and discovery.

For me, the project reinforced the importance of designing experiences that balance engagement with purpose and creating digital interactions that spark imagination while encouraging players to engage with the world around them.
